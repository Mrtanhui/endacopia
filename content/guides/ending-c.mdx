## Requirements checklist

Ending C spans several areas, so confirm the route before advancing the final story. The currently verified checklist includes:

- Collect all **18 fish**.
- Receive the **Old Key** through the fish-related chain.
- Obtain and use the **Projector Remote** where required.
- Follow the hidden map or Projector Room clues.
- In the Office sequence, use telephone number `277-5944`.
- Wait under the stairs for roughly one minute when the route calls for it.
- Complete the later symbol puzzle and do not take a route-ending action too early.

> The Old Key is not the Office Core Key. The Core Key comes from the plant, Wrench, and water-cooler item chain; it should not be substituted in Ending C instructions.
