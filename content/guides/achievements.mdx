## Achievement plan

Steam's official global-achievement page lists 16 achievements. Names and descriptions should always be taken from that page; the walkthrough source is used for chapter placement, missable warnings, and efficient order.

Do not rely on unlock percentages copied into old guides. They change as more players finish the game. The useful permanent information is the condition itself and where it sits in a route.

## Boss outcome achievements

Three major encounters are especially important for planning because the game recognizes aggressive and non-aggressive outcomes.

- **Fatal Performance** and **Cut The Act** relate to the Trapezist route.
- **No Strings Attached** confirms defeating the Chameleon, while **Case Closed** confirms an avoid route exists.
- AI-related outcomes include official achievement signals such as **System Override**.

Use a save before a final commitment where possible. The [Trapezist](/characters/trapezist) and [Chameleon](/characters/chameleon) pages explain the route context without mixing the two outcomes.

## Skill and activity achievements

**Footwork Master** requires a soccer score of 100 points. This is a repetition and timing challenge rather than a story branch, so it is usually cleaner to complete during a dedicated session. Watch Mellow's eye position and use the [soccer ball guide](/puzzles/soccer-ball) for the community-tested timing cue.

Other official examples include **A Natural Brawler**. Check the Steam list for exact wording before publishing a checklist or translating a condition; community pages can simplify names in ways that make later searches harder.

## Ending achievements

Endacopia has multiple outcome routes, and Steam includes ending-related achievements. Complete the story once with minimal spoilers, then use [all endings](/endings) to plan additional outcomes. Ending C requires preparation across several areas and should not be treated as a last-minute dialogue choice.

## Clean-up checklist

1. Finish a blind story route and note what unlocked naturally.
2. Compare the remaining official Steam list with the chapter guide.
3. Revisit boss saves for alternate fight or avoid outcomes.
4. Complete skill challenges such as the 100-point soccer target.
5. Prepare collection-heavy requirements before starting Ending C.
6. Recheck Steam after completion rather than trusting an old percentage screenshot.

> The site intentionally avoids claiming that every achievement can be obtained in one uninterrupted run. Route-exclusive outcomes and missable interactions deserve separate saves until a fully verified minimum-run plan exists.
