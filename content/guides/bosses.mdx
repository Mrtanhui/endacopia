## Regional encounters

The three regional routes here lead to **The AI**, **Trapezist** and **The Chameleon**. This is a route directory, not an official exhaustive boss classification. Steam achievement descriptions corroborate their named outcomes.

## Trapezist

The Trapezist closes the major Misery Town performance chain. You can proceed into the conventional fight or use the Chapter 1 **Scalpel** to cut the rope and take the alternate route.

Steam achievements **Fatal Performance** and **Cut The Act** support the two-outcome structure. See the [Trapezist guide](/characters/trapezist) for the plushie, Jester, microphone, crowd, and performance prerequisites.

## The Chameleon

The Chameleon encounter grows from a Timesville nighttime pursuit and use of the camera. The fight outcome is associated with **No Strings Attached**, while **Case Closed** confirms an avoid route.

Use the [Chameleon guide](/characters/chameleon) to separate chase timing from the final outcome. Simply leaving one chase frame should not be treated as a verified pacifist completion.

## The AI and later encounters

The Office route contains the AI encounter. Steam's **System Override** achievement provides an official outcome label. For its surrounding prerequisites, follow the [area walkthrough](/walkthrough).

Names such as Clockey and RODGER occur in wider community descriptions, but that alone does not establish that both belong to a fixed list of five required main bosses. We do not use that unsupported count here.

## Minor and secret encounters

The Fandom index also lists Matchboxer, Tuna, Danu, Nove, Yew, The Mug, Chimeramanticore, Benjamin, and other minor or hidden encounters. A name in the index does not automatically mean the fight is required.

For optional fights, check three things before committing:

1. Whether the room is part of the main route or a secret area.
2. Whether the encounter can be avoided or delayed.
3. Whether a Steam achievement recognizes a particular outcome.

## General combat approach

- Observe a full attack cycle before using a damage window.
- Treat unusual items or environmental actions as possible alternatives to direct combat.
- Keep saves before encounters with paired outcome achievements.
- Use no-damage videos for animation timing, not as the only source for route prerequisites.
- Return to the chapter walkthrough when a boss room is unavailable; the missing step is often outside the arena.
