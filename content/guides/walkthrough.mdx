## Before you begin

This is a route directory: use it to reach a focused puzzle guide when you are stuck. Keep a separate recovery save before a boss or ending commitment, and inspect new inventory items before returning to a puzzle.

**Areas are not chapter numbers.** Misery Town, Timesville and the Office should not be labeled Chapters 2 and 3 just because a walkthrough presents them later. The [community ending guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773635669) describes preparing all three areas in Chapter I.

## Key items that are easy to confuse

The **Core Key** opens the normal Office chain and comes from the water-cooler puzzle. The **Old/Lost Key** comes from the completed fish collection and is used on the hidden window lock. They are separate items with separate requirements.

The **Metal Detector** is a prerequisite for finding both the Fishing Paper and the Projector Remote. If you are in the theater without it, follow the [Timesville detector setup](/puzzles/old-key#get-the-metal-detector) first. Finding the remote does not finish its hidden route: record the projection and continue into the checkerboard room.

## Prepare the secret route

Before finishing the area routes, use the [Secrets guide](/guide/secrets) to check the three hidden encounters. The [Ending C requirements](/endings/ending-c#requirements-checklist) distinguish owning an item from completing its encounter.

Keep a recovery save rather than assuming an earlier room will remain accessible. This directory does not establish which late-game saves can recover every missed interaction.

## Choose an ending

Open the [endings overview](/endings) for the available routes. If you are pursuing the hidden route, compare your three completed landmarks with the [final Ending C checks](/endings/ending-c#final-symbol-sequence-and-commitment) before committing to the usual ending door.

For achievement cleanup, consult the [achievement guide](/guide/achievements) and [boss outcomes](/bosses). Plan mutually exclusive choices around separate saves instead of assuming every outcome fits one run.
