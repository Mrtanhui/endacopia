## Before you begin

Endacopia lets you explore, but its inventory chains often cross several rooms. Save or pause before obvious story commitments, examine new items, and finish optional interactions before moving to a final branch. This overview stays intentionally concise: use the linked puzzle and ending pages when you need an exact input.

- Keep the **red ball** found in the Chapter 1 medical tent; it begins a later Misery Town item chain.
- Check computers, paper clues, and item descriptions rather than guessing codes.
- Several bosses have both fight and non-fight outcomes tied to different achievements.
- Ending C uses requirements collected across more than one area, so treat it as a planned route.

## Prologue

Use the opening sequence to learn how Guardian Angel interactions differ from Mellow's movement. Click environmental targets deliberately, test the inventory when the cursor changes, and exhaust important dialogue. The prologue establishes the House and the logic the later rooms remix.

Your goal is not to clear every mystery immediately. Focus on the interactions the game makes available, collect obvious tools, and watch for changes in Mellow's senses and abilities.

## Chapter 1

Chapter 1 spreads important items across the House and Office-like spaces. Complete the black-and-white clown sequence and the Whack-a-Mole identification challenge to receive the **Scalpel**. In the switch room, reset the six colored switches before entering the verified order described in the [switch puzzle guide](/puzzles/switch-puzzle).

The Office [Core Key](/puzzles/core-key) has its own item chain: fill the Transparent Mug, water the plant, trade the Flower for the Wrench, remove the water-cooler panel, then release the water so the key drops from the pipe. Do not confuse it with the [Old or Lost Key](/puzzles/old-key) used by Ending C.

## Chapter 2

Misery Town combines performance tasks, item trading, and route choices. For the blue inflatable character, use the red ball at the porta-potty game, complete bingo, learn his weakness, then answer with `Age` or `Voice` to receive the plushie.

The Jester, Pierrot, microphone, crowd, and Trapezist form one connected sequence. You can ultimately confront the Trapezist or use the Scalpel to cut the rope and avoid the conventional fight. Henry's section also contains the Floppy Disk and computer record that reveal the Saw box code.

## Chapter 3 and route cleanup

Timesville and the Office bring several earlier clues together. The Chameleon can be defeated or avoided, and each outcome maps to a separate Steam achievement. If you are pursuing Ending C, confirm its cross-area checklist before you pass an irreversible point: fish collection, the Old Key, [Projector Remote and hidden map](/puzzles/projector-remote), the Office telephone clue, waiting condition, and later symbol puzzle all matter.

Use [all endings](/endings) for a spoiler-light comparison, then open [Ending C](/endings/ending-c) only if you want the full trigger chain.

## Achievement-friendly route

A single blind run should prioritize discovery. For cleanup, group achievements by chapter and by mutually exclusive boss outcome. Steam confirms 16 achievements; the [achievement guide](/guide/achievements) keeps official names separate from community route advice and avoids publishing dynamic unlock percentages as permanent facts.
