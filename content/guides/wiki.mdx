## What is Endacopia?

Endacopia is a surreal point-and-click puzzle adventure by Andyland. You guide Mellow, a lost child, as his Guardian Angel. What looks like a colorful early-computer learning game gradually reveals abstract puzzles, unsettling characters, first-person fights, minigames, and hidden routes.

The game launched on Steam on July 27, 2026. Steam lists it as a single-player title with 16 achievements. Current official store information takes priority whenever it conflicts with older development plans or community summaries.
