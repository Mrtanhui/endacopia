## Get the Metal Detector first

The Projector Remote is buried, so this route cannot be completed until you have the **Metal Detector** from Timesville. Keep it when you return to Misery Town. The [Metal Detector prerequisites](/puzzles/old-key#get-the-metal-detector) cover the Telescope and fossil-shack setup.

The movie tent is the theater near the Circus entrance with eight colored seats and the film clue used by the earlier Whack-a-Mole sequence.

## Dig up the Projector Remote

Enter the Misery Town movie tent and use the Metal Detector on the sandy floor. Search inside the theater rather than the exterior path. The buried item is the **Projector Remote**.

If nothing appears, verify that you are in the colored-seat movie tent and that the Metal Detector is selected as the active inventory item.

## Reveal and save the hidden map

Use the Projector Remote on the projector or screen. The new image is a path map for the black-and-white checkerboard or mime room.

Take a screenshot or copy the route before leaving. The picture is an instruction, not a background Easter egg, and guessing the path can send you back to the room entrance.

## Follow the checkerboard route

1. Start at the entrance of the black-and-white mime room, the room used to retrieve the **Hands**, near the blue clown. The theater and this room are different locations.
2. Keep your captured projector map beside the game and follow its continuous line from the entrance. Use the map's orientation rather than converting it into a remembered series of left/right turns.
3. Continue through the hidden passage. After the altered area fades to white and returns, look for the stairs at the lower-right.
4. Descend and complete the capsule/body minigames. For the mouse-boundary step, switch to windowed mode so the pointer can move outside the game window.
5. Continue until the hidden encounter concludes and you return to Misery Town. Finding the remote or entering the passage alone is not completion.

The [achievement walkthrough](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3774754099) describes the fade, stair and capsule landmarks. The [all-endings guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773635669) identifies the bowling-pin-like figure as this route's completion check.

Complete the hidden route before resolving the Trapezist sequence and closing Misery Town if possible.

This route is one part of the wider secret chain. Use the [Old Key guide](/puzzles/old-key) for the fishing requirement and the [Ending C walkthrough](/endings/ending-c) before making an irreversible ending choice.


## Check each route milestone

| Checkpoint | What should be different |
| --- | --- |
| Remote recovered | The inventory contains the Projector Remote, not just the Metal Detector. |
| Projection activated | The screen shows the route you will follow in the Hands room. Record it before leaving. |
| Map followed | You reach the hidden passage instead of resetting to the entrance. |
| Hidden sequence finished | The bowling-pin-like encounter concludes and you return to Misery Town. |

Return to the [three-area completion check](/guide/secrets#check-what-you-have-actually-completed) after the final milestone. ## If the route does not open

| Symptom | Next check |
| --- | --- |
| Detector finds nothing | Search inside the theater with eight colored seats; confirm the detector is selected. |
| Normal film still showing | Use the recovered remote on the projector/screen before recording the map. |
| Back at the mime-room entrance | Restart from the entrance and compare the next segment with your screenshot. Avoid resuming halfway from memory. |
| Unsure where to go after the passage | Check the white fade and lower-right stair landmark in the cited walkthrough. |
| Pointer cannot cross the required boundary | Switch to windowed mode for that minigame, then retry. This is separate from the Timesville window-lock puzzle. |
| No final secret encounter | Continue past the capsule puzzles; collecting the remote is only the start. |

There is no tested text-only turn sequence here. Use the in-game projection for the exact tiles; this page's diagram explains the locations and checkpoints without pretending to reproduce that map.
