## Get the Metal Detector first

The Projector Remote is buried, so this route cannot be completed until you have the **Metal Detector** from Timesville. Keep it when you return to Misery Town.

If the movie tent has not been used yet, complete its normal clown-film interaction first. It is the theater near the Circus entrance with eight colored seats and the film clue used by the earlier Whack-a-Mole sequence.

## Dig up the Projector Remote

Enter the Misery Town movie tent and use the Metal Detector on the sandy floor. Search inside the theater rather than the exterior path. The buried item is the **Projector Remote**.

If nothing appears, verify that you are in the colored-seat movie tent and that the Metal Detector is selected as the active inventory item.

## Reveal and save the hidden map

Use the Projector Remote on the projector or screen. The new image is a path map for the black-and-white checkerboard or mime room.

Take a screenshot or copy the route before leaving. The picture is an instruction, not a background Easter egg, and guessing the path can send you back to the room entrance.

## Follow the checkerboard route

Return to the black-and-white checkerboard room where you previously obtained the **Hands**. Follow the projected path exactly until you reach the hidden crack or exit. Continue through the corridor and downstairs, then complete the body and surgery minigames. One mouse-boundary puzzle in this sequence also requires windowed mode.

Complete the hidden route before resolving the Trapezist sequence and closing Misery Town if possible.

This route is one part of the wider secret chain. Use the [Old Key guide](/puzzles/old-key) for the fishing requirement and the [Ending C walkthrough](/endings/ending-c) before making an irreversible ending choice.
