## Who is the Chameleon?

Community references place the Chameleon in Timesville and identify it as a major boss. Its encounter combines a nighttime pursuit, visual changes, and a later decision about whether to complete the conventional fight.

The broad identity and tactics come from the community index and gameplay. Steam's achievement list supplies the strongest official confirmation that two distinct outcomes exist.

## Nighttime pursuit

During the pursuit, pay attention to where the Chameleon appears and how the route teaches use of the **camera**. The camera is a defensive or deterrent tool in this sequence, not simply a collectible.

Do not waste time trying unrelated inventory items when the encounter is asking for visual timing. Create space, face the threat, and use the camera according to the established pattern before moving to safety.

## Fight outcome

The conventional encounter leads to defeating the Chameleon. Steam's official achievement **No Strings Attached** confirms that this outcome exists.

Community combat material describes changing appearance and multiple attack behaviors. Learn the tells first, then act during safe windows. A no-damage boss compilation is useful for visual timing, but the written route remains the clearer source for prerequisites.

## Avoid outcome

Steam's **Case Closed** confirms an alternate outcome that avoids the standard defeat route. The exact setup belongs to the Timesville walkthrough state; do not assume simply fleeing any chase frame is sufficient.

Keep a save before the final encounter choice if achievement completion matters. One outcome should not be described as the only “correct” route when the official achievement structure recognizes both.

## Troubleshooting

- If the camera interaction seems unavailable, verify that the chase has reached the correct phase.
- If the Chameleon returns immediately, check whether the visual timing cue was missed.
- If an achievement does not match your intention, confirm whether you completed the fight or the avoid state.
- If you are following a cut video, compare the preceding room setup with a written Chapter 3 guide.
