## Which Jester do players mean?

Searches for “Endacopia Jester” usually point to the **Red Jester**, a Misery Town booth manager and announcer associated with the name Ticketo in community material. He should not be merged with **Pierrot**, who has a separate role in the connected gift sequence.

That distinction matters for walkthroughs: advice aimed at Pierrot will not move the booth state by itself, and a character summary that combines them makes the Trapezist route harder to follow.

## Booth and announcer role

The Red Jester controls or occupies a key booth at Misery Town's entrance and participates in the performance setup. Community references describe his relationship to Pierrot and his function as an obstacle before the crowd can be handled correctly.

Exhaust the relevant dialogue and inspect what each performer wants. The solution is an item and relationship chain rather than an insult, combat action, or random code.

## Plushie and gift sequence

First obtain the plushie through the blue inflatable character's route. That chain uses the red ball from the medical-tent sequence, a porta-potty bingo game, a weakness clue from the other inflatable man, and the response named by that save’s weakness clue (`age`, `smell` or `voice`).

Use the plushie in Pierrot's gift-related sequence. Once the connected request is satisfied, the Red Jester can leave the booth and the route can advance.

## Microphone and crowd

After the booth state changes, obtain or use the microphone as directed by the Misery Town route. Its function is tied to moving or clearing the crowd so the performance can proceed.

The performance eventually leads to the Trapezist outcome. If you collected the earlier Scalpel, you can choose an alternate rope-cutting resolution instead of the standard fight.

## Common mix-ups

- Red Jester and Pierrot are different characters.
- The blue inflatable puzzle supplies an item for this chain but is not the same puzzle.
- The microphone matters after the relationship and booth steps; using it too early does not replace them.
- The clown puzzle in the earlier clown sequence is a different search intent despite the shared circus imagery.
