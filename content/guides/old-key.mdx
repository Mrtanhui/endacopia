## What the Old or Lost Key is for

The fish reward opens a hidden safe-like window on the Ending C route. A Steam puzzle guide calls it the **Lost Key**, while player discussions commonly call it the **Old Key**. This page uses both names and targets the wording players currently search. It is **not** the Office Core Key from the water-cooler puzzle.

“Ancient Key” also appears in search data, but it is not a verified Endacopia item name. If that is what you searched, you probably mean this fish-related Old or Lost Key. Do not start the Office item chain if this is the key your Ending C checklist is missing.

## Find the Fishing Paper

Obtain the **Metal Detector** in Timesville, then use it on the beach to uncover the **Fishing Paper**. The paper tracks the collection that produces the key; finding it does not give you the key immediately.

## Catch all 18 unique fish

The fishing collection is split across six world states: Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, Night 1, Night 2, and Night 3. Each state has three unique fish, for a total of **18**.

- Fish during every day and night state instead of repeating one period.
- Check the Fishing Paper after each set so you can see which slots remain.
- Keep a save before late route commitments in case a missing period is difficult to revisit.
- Find the Fishing Paper before finishing the collection; the sources do not clearly confirm that earlier catches always backfill.

After all 18 slots are complete, inspect or click the Fishing Paper. The **Old or Lost Key** should drop for you to collect.

## Open the hidden window safe

Complete the linked puzzle across the two similar rooms and collect the **VHS Tape**. Return to the smaller room, wait for the reversed voice to finish, then leave the room.

Switch Endacopia to windowed mode with `Alt+Enter`, then enter the smaller room again. What appears as a black opening in fullscreen becomes a large lock in windowed mode. Use the Old or Lost Key on that lock to continue the hidden route.

Finish this sequence before closing the later Chameleon route if possible, because Timesville access can become unavailable.

## If the key does not appear

- Confirm the Fishing Paper shows 18 different fish, not repeated catches.
- Recheck all three day states and all three night states.
- Inspect the completed Fishing Paper instead of only viewing it in inventory.
- Confirm you returned to the smaller of the two similar rooms and waited for its audio cue.
- Make sure you are using the Old or Lost Key on the window safe, not trying it on the Office Core door.
