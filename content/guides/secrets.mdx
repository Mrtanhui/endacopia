## What counts as a secret?

Endacopia hides more than collectible jokes. Some optional interactions reveal maps or rooms, some open alternate boss outcomes, and others feed directly into a hidden ending. This page groups them by consequence rather than pretending every Easter egg has equal weight.

> If you want a blind first run, stop here. The sections below name late-game areas, route items, and Ending C conditions.

## Route-changing secrets

Several apparently small items become route tools later. The **Scalpel** from the Chapter 1 clown sequence can change the Trapezist outcome. The [Projector Remote](/puzzles/projector-remote) and hidden map information in Chapter 2 connect to later discovery. The [Old or Lost Key](/puzzles/old-key) belongs to the Ending C chain and is not the same as the [Core Key](/puzzles/core-key) found through the Office water-cooler puzzle.

Boss encounters also hide alternate outcomes. Steam achievement pairs confirm that the Trapezist, Chameleon, and AI sequences can reward different approaches. Check the [boss archive](/bosses) before committing if you are planning achievement cleanup.

## Ending C clue trail

Ending C is the largest connected secret in the current guide. Community sources describe a chain that includes all 18 fish, the Old Key, Projector Room actions, an Office telescope number, a waiting condition under the stairs, and a later symbol puzzle.

The Office phone number is `277-5944`, and the stair condition requires waiting for roughly a minute. Those details are useful only inside the full route, so use the dedicated [Ending C walkthrough](/endings/ending-c) for order and irreversible warnings.

## How to search without wasting a run

- Revisit previously quiet rooms after receiving a remote, key, tool, or sense.
- Read computer files and paper clues before entering a nearby code.
- Keep a note of fish and optional encounters instead of trusting memory.
- Finish local exploration before advancing an ending branch.
- Treat video compilations as visual confirmation, not as the sole source for a trigger.

## What is still being verified

“Lost key” can describe more than one route in casual searches, so the site now keeps separate [Old or Lost Key](/puzzles/old-key) and [Core Key](/puzzles/core-key) answers instead of merging them into one misleading route. The guide also does not turn every background reference from a secrets compilation into a confirmed mechanic. Only route facts supported by a written guide or official achievement signal are presented as instructions.
