## Choose the secret you are working on

The three area secrets feed the hidden Ending C route. Use this table to find the required item and the next guide without replaying unrelated puzzles.

| Area | Start with | Player task | Detailed steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Misery Town | Metal Detector | Find the Projector Remote, reveal the map, and follow the checkerboard route. | [Projector Remote and hidden map](/puzzles/projector-remote) |
| Timesville | Fishing Paper and all 18 fish | Collect the Old/Lost Key, then use it in the small room in windowed mode. | [Old Key and fish table](/puzzles/old-key) |
| The Office | Telescope | Find the telephone clue, follow its instruction under the stairs, then complete the symbol sequence. | [Ending C: Office instructions](/endings/ending-c#office-telephone-and-waiting-condition) |

These routes are grouped by **area**, rather than using walkthrough section numbers as game chapters. The [Steam Ending C guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773635669) places preparation for all three area secrets in Chapter I.

## Prepare a save before the area bosses

Keep a separate save before finishing each area's boss progression if you intend to complete the secret route. This gives you a way back if an item or interaction is missing. Do not assume that every late-game save can revisit every room.

Use the [Ending C walkthrough](/endings/ending-c) for the combined requirements. The [boss archive](/bosses) covers the separate fight and non-fight outcomes; those choices should not be confused with completing an area's hidden secret.

## Misery Town: remote and hidden map

Bring the Timesville Metal Detector into the movie tent and search the sandy floor. Use the buried Projector Remote to reveal a path, record that path, then follow it in the checkerboard room where you obtained the Hands.

The [Projector Remote guide](/puzzles/projector-remote) explains the location and continuation. Check the [Steam route description](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773635669) if the room state differs from your save.

## Timesville: fish collection and window lock

The Fishing Paper tracks 18 unique fish across six day and night states. Completing it and inspecting it again gives the Old or Lost Key. This is not the Office Core Key.

After completing the shared puzzle between the two similar rooms, return to the smaller one. Switching to windowed mode and re-entering reveals the lock. Use the [Old Key guide](/puzzles/old-key) for the fish table, numbered actions and troubleshooting.

The paper and window-lock steps are illustrated in the [Timesville secret guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773067527).

## The Office: clue, waiting condition and symbols

Use the telescope near the Office entrance to find the telephone clue. Call the number through the Jobs application and follow the message directing you under the stairs. Wait there for about a minute, then continue with the revealed puzzles.

The [community Ending C guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773635669) supplies the clue route and symbol solutions. Our [Office section](/endings/ending-c#office-telephone-and-waiting-condition) connects the steps in one place.

If your current problem is getting through the normal Office item chain, the [Core Key and water cooler guide](/puzzles/core-key) covers Mug → Flower → Wrench → Core Key. Obtaining that key alone does not complete the Office secret.

## Optional interactions and achievement cleanup

Other optional discoveries affect fights and achievements rather than serving as a fourth area secret. The Scalpel from the [clown puzzle](/puzzles/clown-puzzle) can change the later Trapezist encounter. Compare the [boss routes](/bosses) and [achievement guide](/guide/achievements) before choosing an outcome you want to record.

A video compilation can help recognize a room or visual Easter egg. It does not by itself prove that a background reference unlocks an item, achievement or ending. This page does not claim to list every decorative reference in the game.

## Check what you have actually completed

- Finding the remote is not the same as finishing its hidden map route.
- Catching every fish is not the same as collecting and using the key.
- Getting the Office Core Key is not the same as completing the telephone and symbol route.
- Completing one area secret is not enough for Ending C.

If you know the area but not the missing action, start with that row in the table. If all three routes appear complete, continue with the [final Ending C checks](/endings/ending-c#final-symbol-sequence-and-commitment).
