## Exact steps

1. Return to the **porta-potty on the right side of the Misery Town entrance**.
2. Insert the **red ball** collected from the Chapter 1 medical tent.
3. Complete the bingo interaction by lining up five balls.
4. Talk to the other inflatable man and ask about the blue character's weakness.
5. Return to the blue inflatable character.
6. When he asks you to insult him, enter `Age` or `Voice`.
7. Collect the **plushie** after he cries.

The answer is not a random insult. The game expects one of the two weakness words after the clue-gathering state has been completed.

## Where the red ball comes from

The red ball is found earlier in the Chapter 1 medical tent. It can look like a local toy, but it is required for the Misery Town porta-potty game. If the bingo interaction will not begin, check your inventory and earlier room cleanup before searching for a different ball.

This cross-chapter dependency is why the walkthrough flags the ball as a keep item.

## Completing bingo

Insert the red ball, then continue the game until five balls form the required line. Do not leave after the first successful-looking animation; the next dialogue state depends on actually completing bingo.

Once the game is complete, speak with the **other inflatable man**. That conversation provides the weakness information the final input checks.

## Correct response

Enter either `Age` or `Voice` when the blue character requests an insult. Capitalization should not be treated as a substitute for missing prerequisites—if both words fail, revisit the bingo and weakness conversation.

The resulting plushie feeds into Pierrot, the Red Jester, the crowd, and the Trapezist performance route.

## If it does not work

- Confirm the red ball came from the Chapter 1 medical tent.
- Finish the five-ball bingo rather than merely starting it.
- Ask the other inflatable character about the weakness.
- Use a single verified word: `Age` or `Voice`.
- Check whether you already collected or used the plushie in the later chain.
