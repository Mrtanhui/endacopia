## Correct order

The Office or Gambling Den six-color switch puzzle uses this sequence:

1. Set every switch to **off**, leaving only **Yellow on**.
2. Turn **Yellow off**.
3. Turn **Dark Blue on**.
4. Turn **Light Blue on**.
5. Turn **Purple on**.
6. Turn **Red on**.

In shorthand: reset to Yellow only, then `Yellow off → Dark Blue → Light Blue → Purple → Red`.

## Why the reset matters

Entering the color order from an unknown board state can fail even when the last four switches are correct. Begin by turning everything off and deliberately placing Yellow in the starting on state.

Then switch Yellow off as the first input. The reset is part of the solution, not optional cleanup.

## Identifying the two blues

The most common visual mistake is reversing **Dark Blue** and **Light Blue**. Dark Blue comes first. Pause long enough to compare the two switch colors before entering the final sequence.

If display brightness makes them difficult to separate, use their positions from the linked gameplay reference rather than guessing based on saturation alone.

## Completion signal

After Red is turned on in the correct state, the connected mechanism or door should respond. If nothing happens:

- Reset the entire board again.
- Confirm Yellow was the only switch on at the start.
- Turn Yellow off before touching either blue.
- Enter Dark Blue before Light Blue.
- Make sure no unlisted color remained on.

## Route context

This Chapter 1 puzzle is associated with the Office path and the search phrase “Find Your Mouth” in video guides. It is not the same as a later symbol puzzle on the Ending C route.
