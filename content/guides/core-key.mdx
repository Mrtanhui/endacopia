## Reach the item chain

Complete the Office telephone objective, then use the elevator reassessment and choose the money-focused answer to reach the Gambling Den route. When you leave the elevator, collect the **Transparent Mug** on the right.

This sequence produces the **Core Key**. It is separate from the fishing-related Old or Lost Key used for Ending C.

## Water the Office plant

Return to the Office and fill the Transparent Mug at the water cooler. Use the filled mug on the plant to the left. The watered plant gives you a **Flower**.

If the plant does not react, confirm the mug was filled at the cooler and that the telephone and elevator progression has opened the correct Office state.

## Trade the Flower for the Wrench

Go back toward the route's train-station or starting area. Give the Flower to the person holding the **Wrench**, then take the Wrench back to the Office.

The Wrench is the tool for the cooler panel. Do not spend time trying unrelated inventory items on the pipe.

## Release the Core Key

Use the Wrench on the front panel of the Office water cooler. Once the panel is removed, turn the water on again. The **Core Key** drops from the exposed pipe and can be collected.

The key may not grant immediate access to every Core interaction. Use the Wrench on the left-side glass, speak with the other employees, and raise Office productivity. If you won **Airplane Tickets** from the Gambling Den dice game, give them to Malia before treating the route as blocked.

## Core Key or Old Key?

- **Core Key** — Transparent Mug → water → plant → Flower → Wrench → water cooler.
- **Old or Lost Key** — Fishing Paper → 18 unique fish → hidden window safe.

Use the [Old Key guide](/puzzles/old-key) if your objective mentions fish, a black-and-white room, or Ending C. Return to the [full walkthrough](/walkthrough) for the surrounding chapter order.
