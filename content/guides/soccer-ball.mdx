## Official target

Steam's official achievement page states that **Footwork Master** requires reaching a soccer score of **100 points**. That is the stable target; community tips help with execution but do not redefine the achievement.

Treat this as a timing challenge. Clicking faster is not automatically better, and a long streak is easier when you follow one visual cue consistently.

## Visual timing cue

Players on Andyland's official itch.io demo page recommend watching Mellow's eye position. As the eyes move from the center toward the bottom, click the ball to continue the kick.

This is a community-discovered technique, not a formal developer control description. Gameplay footage of 100 kicks supports using a steady visual rhythm rather than frantic input.

## Building the streak

1. Position the cursor where you can reacquire the ball quickly.
2. Watch Mellow's face instead of only tracking the ball arc.
3. Click as the eyes move down from their middle position.
4. Return to the same rhythm after every successful kick.
5. Continue until the score reaches 100.

Avoid changing technique halfway through a good run. The challenge becomes more about concentration than discovery after the first few successful kicks.

## Common mistakes

- Clicking on a fixed timer without watching the animation.
- Double-clicking and consuming an input too early.
- Following the ball alone when its motion becomes harder to judge.
- Stopping at 100 physical clicks rather than confirming the on-screen score reached 100.

## Achievement cleanup advice

Complete Footwork Master during a separate cleanup session rather than in the middle of a story route. It has no known reason to justify risking an unsaved branch or interrupting a longer puzzle chain.

If the eye cue feels inconsistent, compare your game's current animation and frame pacing with the linked gameplay video instead of forcing the demo-era timing exactly.
